A "Nord" or "Catppuccin" Dark Palette: Deep slate or charcoal backgrounds (#1e1e2e), crisp white/off-white text for readability, and subtle, intentional accents (like a single muted purple or mint green) to highlight active states or key metrics.

Monospace Typography: Using high-quality system mono fonts (like JetBrains Mono, Fira Code, or SF Mono) for data, codes, or status labels to give it a sharp, technical feel.

Border-Based Structure: Instead of heavy drop shadows or flashy gradients, using crisp 1px borders (border: 1px solid #313244) to separate sections, reminiscent of a tiled window manager layout.
Keep mobile screens in mind: On mobile, the borders and shadows can be reduced or eliminated to create a more compact and streamlined design.
